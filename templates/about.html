About

Built for independent music creators.

Cadence is a TikTok scheduling product for independent music artists, indie labels, and artist managers. Each creator connects their own TikTok account, uploads videos they own the rights to, writes captions, and schedules posts around their release calendar.

It is made by Bigger Dreams, a Los Angeles music-and-video team behind the live-session series Wish You Were Here. We built Cadence after living the timing crunch of release weeks — and opened it up for any independent creator to use.

Any eligible creator can sign up, connect their own account, and start scheduling. Cadence only ever posts a creator's own content to their own TikTok.
